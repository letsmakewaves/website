# Study log: reference creator videos

Patterns learned from talking videos the user sends. We learn general performance habits (timing, expression, gestures), never a person's identity, face, script or exact routine.

**How to log a video:**
1. Pull frames at 2 per second (contact sheet), and note the length and framing.
2. If the audio is clear, transcribe it so beats can be matched to words.
3. Fill in one entry using the format below.
4. When a pattern shows up in **2 or more videos**, promote it into `performance-library.md` (intent table, gestures, density rules or anti-patterns) and mark it as promoted here.

**Entry format:**
- **Video n:** source, length, framing, energy
- **Beats seen:** words or moment, then face / head / hands / body
- **Gesture count:** gestures per 10 seconds, and where they peaked
- **New patterns:** anything not yet in the library
- **Confirms:** library rules this video supports
- **Promoted:** yes or no, and where

## Video 1: @thejuliverse talking head (about 10s)
- **Framing:** mid-torso, 9:16, warm room with plants and window-light shadows; conversational energy.
- **Beats seen:**
  - **Opening lines:** relaxed neutral with a slight smile; head mostly square.
  - **Point being made:** **both hands rise briefly** to chest height, fingers loosely spread, then drop back out of frame. This is the only gesture in the clip.
  - **A cheeky or sceptical line:** **head tilts** to one side, one brow up, lips pressed into a half-smile.
  - **Good-news lines:** **wide, real smile** with teeth, cheeks lifting; the smile comes and goes with the words.
  - **Between beats:** returns to a calm, pleasant neutral; small head drift; natural blinks.
- **Gesture count:** about 1 gesture per 10s, at chest height, with both hands because neither is holding anything.
- **New patterns:** the smile is word-triggered, not constant; the hands are out of frame at rest, so a gesture "enters" and leaves.
- **Confirms:** the face carries the delivery; at most 2 gestures per 10s; neutral between beats; mid-torso framing keeps gestures inside the frame.
- **Promoted:** yes. Intent table (cheeky truth, offer, punchline), density rules, framing note.

## Video 2: @thejuliverse "Day 5" fashion/AI tutorial (77s; about 35s talking, the rest B-roll)
- **Framing:** mid-torso, 9:16, bright apartment with soft window light. Jump cuts between sentences, some cuts punched in tighter. Energy: upbeat, conversational-to-hype.
- **Beats seen:**
  - **Greeting:** holds two fabric props up on either side of her face, then a **friendly open-palm wave** on the hello, with a big smile.
  - **"THIS is powerful":** **one index finger raised** at chest height on "THIS" (emphasis, not pointing at the camera); the other hand loosely open.
  - **"imagine":** **hand to chin**, head tilted, eyes drifting up and to the side, a thoughtful half-smile.
  - **"you can actually SHOW them":** **open palm reaching toward the camera**, brows up, eyes wide, mouth open in excitement.
  - **Plain explaining stretches:** hands drop out of frame, face sincere, small head drift, several seconds with no gesture.
  - **Making a point:** **slow blink / eyes closing for a beat** mid-sentence, then reopening on the key word.
  - **"comment prompt":** eyes close briefly with a smile.
  - **"Create yours":** **brief point toward the camera** at the bottom of the frame on "yours".
  - **"DAY 5 DONE":** **thumb or point back at herself**, a proud smile.
  - **"Day six":** **one finger up** for the number, the other hand open, brows raised.
  - **Ending:** a warm, full smile, a small nod, no hands.
- **Gesture count:** about 9 gestures in about 35s of talking (about 2.5 per 10s at this upbeat energy). **Almost every gesture lands on the word the captions highlight** ("THIS", "SHOW", "prompt", "yours", "DONE", "Six").
- **New patterns:**
  - Gestures sit on the one keyword per sentence.
  - Hand to chin on "imagine".
  - A slow blink or brief eye close for emphasis.
  - One finger up for a number or "this".
  - A brief point at the camera only on "you / yours" in a CTA.
  - A thumb back at herself on "I / me / done".
  - An open-palm wave on the greeting.
  - Props held either side of the face to open.
  - Jump cuts with punch-ins between sentences.
- **Confirms (also seen in video 1):**
  - The face carries the delivery.
  - The smile comes with good-news words, and she returns to neutral between beats.
  - Hands rest out of frame and "enter" for a gesture.
  - Mid-torso framing keeps gestures in frame.
  - Gestures land on the key point of the line.
- **Promoted:** yes.
  - **Keyword rule:** gestures land on the line's keyword.
  - **Density** now depends on energy.
  - The **anti-pattern for pointing and counting** has been corrected: allowed on a real number or a CTA "you".
  - **Candidates** (seen once, waiting for a second video): hand to chin on "imagine", slow-blink emphasis, thumb back at self, greeting wave, props either side of the face, punch-in jump cuts.
