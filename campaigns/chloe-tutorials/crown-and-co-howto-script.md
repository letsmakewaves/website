# Chloe tutorial: how I made the Crown & Co. hair ad (about 55 seconds)

Format: Chloe talks (made with Gemini Omni Flash, voice via chloe2), and while she explains each step, your screen recording plays on screen with Chloe cut out in the bottom corner.
Omni prompts for every clip: `crown-and-co-howto-omni-prompts.txt` (attach only the atelier image).

## 1. The script

| # | Time | Chloe says | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|

Every line is about 20 words, so each Omni clip is 10 seconds and she doesn't rush or stutter.

## 2. What to screen record (do this first)

Record each one as a separate short clip (10–20 seconds each; you'll speed them up and trim them in CapCut).

- **A. Claude:** a new chat, typing something like: *"Plan a 20-second hair ad for my wig brand Crown & Co. 5 wigs, luxury, model in a black logo tee, charcoal studio."* Then scroll slowly through the reply.
- **B. Gemini:** paste one image prompt, wait for the result, then scroll through the 5 finished looks.
- **C. Higgsfield:** upload an image → choose Kling 3.0 → paste the motion prompt → set 5 seconds → generate → play the finished clip.
- **D. Claude edit:** ask Claude to "cut the clips to the beat of this song and end on the flyer", then play the finished Reel.

How to record:
- **iPhone:** Control Centre → Screen Recording button (add it under Settings → Control Centre if missing).
- **Android:** pull down the quick settings → Screen recorder.
- **Windows:** Win + Alt + R (Xbox Game Bar), or Snipping Tool → Record.
- **Mac:** Cmd + Shift + 5 → Record selected portion.

Before you record:
- Zoom the browser in (Ctrl/Cmd and +) so the text is big enough to read on a phone.
- Close other tabs, and hide your email, credit balance, and anything personal.
- Move the mouse slowly. Fast scrolling looks messy once it's shrunk to a phone screen.

## 3. Putting it together in CapCut (step by step)

1. **New project, 9:16.** Import the 6 Chloe clips (with the chloe2 voice), the 4 screen recordings, and the Crown & Co. Reel.
2. **Main track:** put the 2-second Reel hook first, then Chloe clips 1 to 6 in order. Her voice now runs the whole video.
3. **Add the recordings:** drag recording A onto the track above Chloe clip 2 (Overlay). Repeat with B over clip 3, C over clip 4, D over clip 5, and the full Reel over clip 6.
4. **Fit each recording:** select it and stretch it to fill the top 2/3 of the screen. Desktop recordings are wide, so zoom in on the important part (the prompt box, the result) and crop the rest.
5. **Match the length:** trim or speed up each recording (Speed → 1.5x–3x) so it ends exactly where that Chloe clip ends.
6. **Put Chloe in the corner:** select the Chloe clip under each recording → **Remove background** (Cutout → Auto removal). Then move it to the top layer (Overlay), shrink it to about 40%, and place it bottom-left or bottom-right. It looks like she's standing in front of the screen.
   - Option if the cutout flickers: use **Mask → Circle** instead, so Chloe sits in a round bubble in the corner.
7. **Zoom on the key moments:** on each recording, add keyframes to push in on the prompt being pasted and on the result appearing. Movement keeps people watching.
8. **Step titles:** add the text for each step (STEP 1: PLAN WITH CLAUDE, and so on) at the top, bold, white with a black outline, shown for the first 2–3 seconds of each step only. These are labels, not subtitles.
9. **Music:** add a soft beat under everything at about 10–15% volume so her voice stays clear. Turn the screen recordings' own sound off.
10. **Export:** 1080p, 30fps.
