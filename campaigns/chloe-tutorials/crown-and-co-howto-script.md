## What to screen record (on your phone, like her)

Record on your **phone** at claude.ai (or the Claude app) so it fits the 9:16 video without cropping. Phone recordings look exactly like hers.

1. **Claude brief:** open a new chat, type *"Plan a 20-second hair ad for my wig brand Crown & Co. 5 wigs, luxury, model in a black logo tee, charcoal studio,"* send, and scroll **slowly** through the reply.
2. **Gemini:** paste one image prompt, wait for the result, then scroll through the 5 looks.
3. **Higgsfield / Kling:** upload a look, paste the motion prompt, set 5s, generate, and play the clip.
4. **The edit:** the message asking Claude to cut the clips to the beat, then the finished Reel playing.

Phone recording: iPhone, Control Centre → Screen Recording. Android, quick settings → Screen recorder.
Before recording: turn on Do Not Disturb, set the battery and time to look clean, and hide your credit balance and personal info.

## The edit in CapCut (to match her look)

1. **New project, 9:16.** Main track: Chloe clips 1–4 with the chloe2 voice, then the Crown & Co. Reel at the end.
2. **Punch-in zooms (her signature):** split Chloe's clip at each sentence. Alternate the scale between **100% and 120%** (centred on her face). This gives the medium/close-up jump every few seconds.
3. **Pop-up image:** at "a hair campaign like this", add the honey blonde image as an Overlay. Place it top-right at about 30% size, add a white border and the **"Pop" in-animation**, and keep it for 2–3 seconds.
4. **Screen recording over Chloe:** drag each phone recording onto the Overlay track above clips 2–4. Scale it to about **80% width, centred**, and add rounded corners and a soft shadow. Chloe's video stays visible around it, the same as the reference.
5. **Speed:** speed the recordings up (1.5x–3x) so the scrolling matches what she's saying. Cut out the waiting while things generate.
6. **Cut back to Chloe** for about 1 second in the middle of clip 3 (delete that part of the overlay). It keeps her present.
7. **The payoff:** the Reel plays full screen with its music. Fade Chloe's voice and the music in under it.
8. **Text:** none, apart from your handle. Her video has no subtitles.
9. **Export:** 1080p, 30fps.
