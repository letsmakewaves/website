## If Gemini blocks a prompt ("might violate our policies")

That's the filter, not your idea. Fixes, in order:
1. Remove any wording about skin tone, "bleached", "plucked" or "scalp". (The pack is already cleaned of these.)
2. Use the short version of the prompt.
3. Start a **fresh chat** and attach only Images 1 and 2.
4. Generate without references from the short prompt, then edit the face in: *"Use the face from Image 1, keep everything else."*

## If a wig doesn't look expensive enough

Reply in the same Gemini chat with one of these fixes (they keep everything else):
- **Looks synthetic or plastic:** *"Make the hair look like real raw human hair: soft natural sheen, visible individual strands, no plastic gloss."*
- **Thin or see-through ends:** *"Make the hair fuller: 250% density, thick blunt ends, no thin or wispy ends."*
- **Visible lace edge or a fake hairline:** *"Melt the lace invisibly into her skin and give her a natural-looking hairline with soft baby hairs."*
- **Frizz or flyaways (straight looks):** *"Perfectly sleek, no flyaways, mirror-like glass shine."*
- **Flyaways, frizz halo or stringy waves:** *"Remove all flyaways and the frizz halo; clean smooth silhouette, sealed cuticle with liquid shine, full polished waves, never stringy. Soften the rim light. Keep everything else."*
- **Hair covering the logo:** *"Move the hair on her left side behind her shoulder so the full 'Crown & Co.' logo is visible. Keep everything else."*
- **Dull curls:** *"Juicy, hydrated, defined coils with natural sheen, no frizz."*
