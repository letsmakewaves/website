# Crown & Co. v2: video plan (edit path and motion prompts)

Deliverable: one **9:16 Reel/TikTok, about 25–30 seconds**, cut to music. Each look gets an emotional moment, not just a movement. **Benchmark (updated):** the reference creator's wig collection video the user sent (her Visual Blueprint demo, 0:49–1:05). Her shots start close on the face and the camera reveals the full body.

## 0. What her video does (studied frame by frame)

- **Every look is about 2 seconds,** cut fast. Seven looks in about 16 seconds. The energy comes from the cutting and the music, not from big actions.
- **The camera does the moving.** Most shots open **chest-up on the face**, then the camera smoothly **pulls back to show the full length of the hair and the full body.** Other moves: a slow orbit to profile, a tilt down the length of the hair, a slow push-in.
- **She moves like a high-fashion model:** slow, controlled, every move eased in and out. Never fast, never bouncy, never a big laugh.
- **Her signature beat: eyes down, then up to the lens.** Head slightly bowed, eyes lowered, then she lifts her gaze into the camera with a calm, knowing, closed-lip look.
- **Hands only touch the hair:** fingertips lifting the hair at the temple, a hand in the curls at the crown, fingers running down the ends, a hand on the hip, an arm folded with a hand at the collarbone.
- **Body turns to show the hair:** square to the camera, then a slow turn to profile or three-quarters so the length and back of the hair show, then the head turns back to the camera.
- **A light breeze** keeps the hair alive. The hair moves softly; it never flies around.

## 1. How each clip is made (start and end frame)

Kling 3.0 accepts a **start image and an end image** (checked):
- **Start image = that look's close-up (B).**
- **End image = that look's full-body frame (A).**
- Kling makes the camera move between them while she acts.

**The end frame must be a calm, finished pose** (not mid-spin or mid-flip), with the same light, backdrop, wig and outfit as the close-up. If your A frame is an action pose, make a new one in Gemini as an edit of it: *"Same image, keep everything identical; ONLY change her pose to: [the end pose below]."*

- Cuts land on the beat, so the slow, elegant motion plus fast cutting gives the energy, like her video.
- Look 1's wig name shows at 1.5s, once the full length is visible.
- I join everything with ffmpeg, check the size (1080x1920) with ffprobe, and send it to you before anything is final.
