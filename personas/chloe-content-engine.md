# Chloe content engine

## Positioning
**Chloe is an openly AI creator who makes ads brands pay for, and shows exactly how.**
- **Who she's for:**
  1. Aspiring AI creators, mostly 20–35, in Nigeria and abroad. They become students and community members.
  2. Small brands and business owners: skincare, food, fashion, apps. They become ad clients.
- **What she gets people to do:** comment a keyword for a free prompt pack, which turns into a DM conversation and an email. That leads to done-for-you ads or, later, a course or community.

## Content pillars (repeat weekly)
1. **Made in 10 minutes:** reveal a finished AI ad, then a quick breakdown of how it was made. This builds proof and attracts clients.
2. **Steal my prompt:** one prompt or tool tip with the result. This brings in followers and saves.
3. **Brand glow-up:** "If [type of brand] hired me…", a before-and-after redesign of a product ad. Use fictional brands or clients who have agreed.
4. **Behind Chloe:** how she's made, what she's tried that failed, mistakes, and your live sessions. This builds trust and answers the "she's AI?!" curiosity.

## Lead magnet
**"Chloe's 5 Ad Prompts":** five copy-paste prompts (skincare, food, fashion, app, real estate), each with an example image. CTA: *"Comment PROMPTS and I'll send it to you."* Deliver it with a ManyChat-style automated DM, which collects an email or WhatsApp number.

## Rules
- Every post shows a real result: an ad, a prompt, or a before-and-after. Her looks bring people in; the skill keeps them.
- Chloe's tone is warm, confident and helpful. Never flirty.
- Post brand recreations only as "concept ads" or with permission. Never imply a brand sponsored the post.
- Always use the AI label. Use #ad on any paid brand work.
